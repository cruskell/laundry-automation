# Dosing and maintenance

Selective paraphrases; page numbers refer to printed pages. Inspect original diagrams for button sequences and component placement.

## Automatic dosing

Source: ご使用ガイド pp.2–4, 8; 取扱説明書 pp.13, 23, 30, 34–35, 38.

- Configure each tank separately. Automatic dosing is disabled at purchase; filling tanks alone does not enable it. Check the illuminated dosing indicators after selecting the course.
- Tank 1: liquid laundry detergent, approximately 1010 ml. Tank 2: softener, approximately 890 ml. Tank 3 (`選べるタンク`): one of delicate detergent, oxygen-based liquid bleach, or 汚れはがし剤, approximately 730 ml. Do not exceed the fill marks.
- `基準量` means the product's usage **per 30 L of water**, not its cap size, tank volume, or the amount for one load. Use the actual container/manufacturer's information. The guide's brand examples are dated March 2025 and should not override a changed formulation's label.
- Initial baseline values: detergent/softener 10 ml, delicate detergent 40 ml, bleach 20 ml, 汚れはがし剤 25 ml. These defaults are not evidence that the user's product has been configured correctly.
- Put delicate detergent in tank 3, not tank 1. Chlorine bleach, powdered products, liquid soap, and products without a known dosage are not automatic-dosing products. Powdered soap and baking soda cannot be used in this appliance.
- Course compatibility differs: inspect the guide p.4 table. `＋2度洗い` detergent is automatic only. `汚れはがし` requires the corresponding third-tank agent. `おうちクリーニング` uses delicate detergent, not ordinary detergent. `タオル` does not automatically add softener.
- Bleach/汚れはがし剤 normally must be selected each run; persistence can be configured (manual p.30). Detergent/softener settings are remembered. Do not assume the third tank is enabled just because it contains liquid.
- Same-brand product may be topped up. Before changing brand or third-tank agent type, empty and clean both tank and path, refill, and reconfigure type/baseline. Do not mix brands or agent types.
- A low-level flashing indication calls for prompt refilling; remaining cycles vary. Do not promise a fixed number of washes from that indication.
- For manual dosing, disable the corresponding automatic setting to prevent double dosing. Use the model's indicated load-based dosage and the relevant table, not a guessed water volume. Gel balls, detergent sticks and beads do not go in the dispenser; follow their container instructions (guide p.8).

### Drying and drain filters

Guide p.7; manual p.48.

- Do not remove drying filters during operation. Gently remove lint with a wrung towel; if clogged, rinse with lukewarm water and dry thoroughly. For U04 clean both main and rear filters. Rub the rear filter with finger pads, not a brush. Do not operate with filters removed.
- Before removing the drain filter, run `脱水` to empty the drum. Then turn power on, place a container, and turn the knob. **If the buzzer sounds, do not remove it:** water can overflow. Ensure residual water has stopped before pulling out. Do not force this procedure if a drainage fault prevents emptying; consult the source/service guidance.
- Clean debris, restore any displaced gasket, insert with `ウエ` upward and the knob tip aligned to `○`, then turn to `▶`. A loose filter can cause U18.

### Automatic-dosing tanks and paths

Guide pp.5–6; manual p.35. Open the tap; leave the drum empty.

1. Remove the case/tanks without tipping; wash tanks and lids with approximately 40 °C water until slime is removed. Do not reuse removed detergent. Reassemble matching the component colors.
2. Add approximately 40 °C water to the specified level: tank 1 approximately 500 ml, tank 2 approximately 450 ml, tank 3 approximately 350 ml. Dissolving 2 teaspoons of citric acid improves cleaning; the guide specifies citric acid for well water.
3. From power off, hold the target tank's dosing button while pressing power. Confirm the 4-minute display, then press `スタート/一時停止`. For an actually clogged path, press the same tank button again to select the 45-minute thorough mode, confirm its display, then start. Inspect guide p.6 image before explaining display/button details.
4. At completion remove the tank, discard remaining water and wipe dry. If citric acid was used, rinse the tank. Reinstall tanks and case. Keep the manual-dispenser case installed during operation so automatic tanks remain correctly positioned.

### Drum care: distinguish the products and courses

Manual pp.32–33, visually checked. Tap open, no clothing.

- **No cleaning agent:** `サッと槽すすぎ` approximately 5 minutes before laundry; `槽乾燥` approximately 30 minutes weekly; `約60 ℃槽カビクリーン` approximately 2 hours monthly or on the cleaning indication. Do not add bleach/detergent to these courses.
- **Cleaning agent required:** `槽洗浄` approximately 11 hours or `約30 ℃槽洗浄` approximately 3 hours. For prevention, the manual specifies approximately 200 ml of clothing-use chlorine bleach monthly/on indication. For existing mold/odor, use the entire specified Panasonic drum-cleaner container; verify the product in p.50.
- For those agent-using courses: ventilate and wear rubber gloves, start the selected course, wait until filling finishes, pause, add the specified agent **directly to the drum**, close the door, and resume.
- Oxygen bleach, kitchen-use chlorine bleach, and surfactant-containing commercial drum cleaners can foam excessively and cause premature draining with inadequate cleaning. Do not substitute them.
- Automatic drum care does not remove the need for periodic drum cleaning (manual p.11).
