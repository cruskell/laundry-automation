# Operation and course selection

Selective paraphrases of the supplied manuals. All page references are printed pages.

## Preparation and everyday operation

Source: 取扱説明書 pp.4–6, 14–21; ご使用ガイド p.1.

- Check care labels, empty pockets, remove hair/sand, close zippers, tie strings, and separate color-bleeding or heavily soiled items. Delicate underwear and small items need suitable laundry nets; do not dry items inside nets.
- Open the water tap, load without trapping clothes in the door, and close the door. Keep the tap open during drying too: water flushes lint from the internal drying path. Close it after operation.
- Select `洗濯`, `洗濯～乾燥`, or `乾燥`, then course and dosing settings, then start. Automatic dosing is initially disabled. With manual dosing, add the indicated amount after load measurement/display; avoid double dosing.
- Normal maximums are 12 kg washing/spinning and 6 kg drying, measured as dry fabric weight. Course-specific limits override these maxima. A full 12 kg wash cannot all be dried in one load.
- `予約` means how many hours later the cycle should **finish**, not start (p.21; ranges p.47). The repository's external scheduler is a separate mechanism.
- After start, operation mode/course cannot be changed; some wash/rinse/spin settings can be changed by pausing before washing ends (p.21). For locked doors inspect p.9, including cooling/water restrictions.

## Drying

Source: 取扱説明書 pp.14–15, 26–27.

- `標準` / `省エネ`: compatible everyday clothes. `しっかり`: thick items prone to uneven drying.
- Timer settings: 10, 30, 60, 90, 120 minutes, depending on course. Timed drying is recommended for loads up to 1 kg or partly dry clothes.
- Dry-only limits: おまかせ/わが家流 6 kg; ナイト 4.5 kg; 毛布 3 kg; おうちクリーニング 0.4 kg. Check eligible blanket types and preparation pp.16–17, 27. Do not use the washing cap during drying.
- For fewer creases, reduce load size; the manual suggests 3 kg or less. Remove crease-prone items promptly and hang to finish as directed. Cotton/linen knits and similar items may shrink.
- Do not generalize the special drying exceptions for blankets or おうちクリーニング to ordinary tumble drying. Inspect care symbols and course-specific instructions.
